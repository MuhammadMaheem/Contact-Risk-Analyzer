CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of January 15, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Meridian Retail Group Inc. ("Client"), located at 88 Commerce Plaza, Chicago, IL, and Vantage Point Advisors LLC ("Consultant"), located at 14 Riverside Drive, Denver, CO.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Consultant shall provide supply-chain optimization advisory services to Client as described in Exhibit A (the "Services").

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months, expiring on January 15, 2027 ("Expiry Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 5.

3. PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay Consultant a fixed fee of $180,000, payable in a single lump sum. Notwithstanding the foregoing, all invoices submitted by Consultant shall be due and payable by Client within ninety (90) days of the invoice date. Late payments shall not accrue interest.

4. TERMINATION
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice. Client may terminate immediately for Consultant's material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice.

5. RESPONSIBILITIES
Consultant shall assign a senior advisor to lead the engagement and shall deliver a written report at the midpoint and conclusion of the engagement. Client shall provide Consultant with reasonable access to relevant personnel, systems, and data necessary to perform the Services.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each Party shall keep confidential any proprietary information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Consultant's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the preceding three (3) months.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
