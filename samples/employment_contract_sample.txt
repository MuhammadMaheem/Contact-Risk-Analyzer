EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Solara Biotech Corp. ("Employer"), located at 900 Innovation Way, San Diego, CA, and Jordan Ellis ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee shall serve as Senior Research Scientist and shall perform duties as reasonably assigned by Employer, including laboratory research, data analysis, and reporting to the Head of R&D.

2. COMPENSATION
Employer shall pay Employee an annual base salary of $145,000, payable in accordance with Employer's standard payroll practices, plus eligibility for an annual discretionary bonus.

3. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated by either Party in accordance with Section 6.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
Employee shall devote substantially all working time to the business of Employer, shall comply with all Employer policies, and shall promptly disclose any inventions conceived during employment. Employer shall provide Employee with the equipment, facilities, and support reasonably necessary to perform the assigned duties.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee shall not disclose Employer's trade secrets or confidential business information at any time during or after employment.

6. TERMINATION
Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon fourteen (14) days' written notice.

7. NON-COMPETE
For a period beginning on the Effective Date and continuing indefinitely thereafter, without any geographic limitation, Employee shall not, anywhere in the world, engage in, own, manage, operate, consult for, or be employed by any business that competes, directly or indirectly, with any product or service offered by Employer at any time during Employee's employment.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
